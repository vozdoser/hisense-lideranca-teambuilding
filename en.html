About the program

Leadership that gives direction,
engages and creates safety

The Leadership with Purpose Program was designed for Hisense to strengthen leaders who can give clear direction, communicate with purpose and, through the way they communicate, build workplaces that feel safe and genuinely engaging.

It develops the competencies leadership depends on, feedback, courageous conversations, direction, engagement and relational intelligence, while drawing people together around a shared purpose and helping to lower the barriers of language, culture and relationship.

The program unfolds in two complementary deliveries. First the Leadership with Purpose Workshop, a three-hour session for up to 30 leaders. Then the “We Are Hisense” Team Building, for up to 120 people, which carries the learning across the wider team.

Leadership with Purpose Program
02
The training

The Leadership with Purpose Workshop

3 hours · up to 30 leaders

Built for a group of up to 30 leaders, this three-hour session develops the way leaders communicate and sharpens their awareness of the effect they have on people and on the workplace. Leaders leave with practical strategies to direct, engage and lead their teams with more awareness, more confidence and better results.

What we work on
  • Building emotionally safe workplaces through communication
  • The Leadership with Purpose formula
  • Communication with Conscious Impact
  • Feedback techniques and strategies
  • Hands-on feedback exercises and simulations
  • Strategies and simulations for courageous conversations
  • Relational Intelligence applied to leadership
Who leads the session

The workshop is led by two Communication Therapists from Voz do Ser, specialists in Leadership with Purpose, and runs entirely in English. Alongside them, a bilingual support team welcomes the group and looks after the participants from start to finish.

Where it happens

Two ways to run the workshop

The Leadership with Purpose Workshop can take place at Hisense or at the Voz do Ser Institute.

In-company, at Hisense

The Voz do Ser team comes to Hisense and runs the session on site, bringing the methodology, the facilitators and the support the experience calls for.

At the Voz do Ser Institute

The 30 leaders are hosted at the Voz do Ser Institute for the full experience, away from the routine and from the workplace, with breakfast, a proper welcome and everything the venue has to offer.

04
The second delivery

The “We Are Hisense” Team Building

4 hours · up to 120 people · three languages

Created for up to 120 people, the “We Are Hisense” Team Building is a four-hour hands-on experience that strengthens integration, belonging and the quality of the relationships inside the company.

Rather than talk about unity, the point is to live it. Through content, group dynamics and shared experiences, the team looks at how they speak to one another, how they relate, and how, together, they build a workplace where people feel safe.

The aim is to bring people closer, take the barriers down, and make it plain that different languages, cultures and roles are no obstacle to moving toward one purpose and speaking with one voice: We Are Hisense.

Content and experiences
  • Building emotionally safe workplaces
  • Mental health and social well-being at work
  • Communication with Conscious Impact
  • Communication with Purpose strategies
  • Relationships with Purpose and stronger bonds
  • Emotional Intelligence and Relational Intelligence
  • Belonging, integration and collaboration dynamics
  • Ways through cultural and relational barriers
  • Hands-on connection and integration activities
  • Building a culture of unity around a shared purpose

An experience for everyone

Language as a bridge,
never a barrier

So that language never gets in the way, Voz do Ser works with Trampolean, a company specialized in technology and Artificial Intelligence.

A technical solution is built for this event in particular, providing simultaneous translation in Portuguese, English and Mandarin throughout, so that everyone in the room can take part, follow what is said and connect with the rest of the group.

Trampolean’s technology and translation setup is already covered by the Team Building budget, so all 120 participants share the same experience, whichever language they speak.
